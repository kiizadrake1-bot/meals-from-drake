About Drakes Foods

We are a warm and welcoming food spot in Kampala Bunamwaya, serving home-style meals with authentic taste and memorable service.

Our story

Drakes Foods began with a simple goal: to serve tasty, nourishing meals that feel like home. Over time, our family-run business grew into a trusted local favorite, known for generous portions, friendly service, and comforting flavors.

We believe food should make people feel cared for, connected, and satisfied. That is why we focus on fresh ingredients, creative menu ideas, and a welcoming customer experience for every guest.

Our values

  • Fresh ingredients and consistent quality
  • Friendly, personal customer service
  • Affordable meals for every occasion
  • Local flavor with a modern touch
